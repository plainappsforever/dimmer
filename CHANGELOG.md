# Changelog

Version scheme: `versionName` MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH, `versionCode` MMmmpp (3.1.0 = 30100). Every upload to Google Play,
including test tracks, needs a higher `versionCode` than the one before.

## 3.1.1 (versionCode 30101) - 2026-10-12
- Play policy audit fixes (`docs/PLAY_POLICY_AUDIT.md` C2-C5): Settings > About > Privacy policy (opens the page in the
  browser; no internet permission); disclosure says "never collects or shares any data. Your settings stay on this
  phone" and shows the "Allow restricted settings" help only for installs outside Google Play; the system-brightness
  method explains that auto-brightness is switched off while dimming and restored afterwards; 512 px store icon as RGBA.
- Translated into 14 languages: German, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), French, Italian, Turkish, Russian, Indonesian,
  Hindi, Japanese, Chinese (Simplified), Korean, Arabic, Vietnamese (`scripts/l10n/`, generated by
  `scripts/make-strings.py`); per-app language in Android 13+ Settings; the five default presets show translated names
  until renamed (stored names stay English for automation).

## 3.1.0 (versionCode 30100) - 2026-10-11
- First Google Play candidate: v3 (hybrid backlight + overlay, presets, tints, schedule, pause, shake to turn off,
  notification, widget, tiles, shortcuts, room light, automation) plus the v3.1 review fixes. See `docs/V3_LOG.md`.
